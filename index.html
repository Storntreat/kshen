About

I’m a Chemical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo (graduating in 2030), passionate about design, processing, and sustainable innovation. I (try to) compete in hackathons and explore the engineering design process, building my skills in this ever-evolving world.


I love tinkering and making things more convenient, whether that means 3D-printing organizational tools or improving small systems. I can make almost any project work and rarely quit when I care about the outcome.
